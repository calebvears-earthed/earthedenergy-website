Three-phase vs Single-phase

Three-phase is common on Hills acreage. It opens the door to bigger systems and closes it on some inverters.

On this page

  1. Single vs three-phase at a glance

How to tell

Meter box · main switch

Open your meter box. A single-phase home has one main switch, rated 63 or 80 A. A three-phase home has a triple main switch, three poles linked by a bar. Properties with ducted aircon, a large shed, a pool, a bore, or built before the 1980s on acreage are often three-phase. Newer suburban estates are almost always single.

If you are not sure, photograph the switchboard and text it to us. We can tell in ten seconds.

What it changes

Export · inverter · backup

SAPN limits are per phase. Single-phase gets 1.5 kW fixed or up to 10 kW flexible. Three-phase gets 4.5 kW fixed or up to 30 kW flexible. That alone makes a 15 kW array sensible on three-phase where it would be wasteful on single.

Inverters are phase-specific. GoodWe ESA is single-phase only. GoodWe ET Plus and Sigenergy SigenStor TP are the three-phase options. Backup on three-phase needs care: a three-phase Sigenergy with Gateway backs up all three phases; some systems only back up one, and your ducted aircon may be on another. We check what sits on which phase at the site visit.
