What it does

Four things a solar-integrated charger does that a plug-in charger does not

Charges on surplus only

Solar-priority mode watches your export and feeds exactly that into the car, so the battery and house come first. On a 10 kW array that is typically 3 to 6 kW into the car through the middle of the day.

Adds 8 to 12 kWh a day to your load

An EV doing 40 km a day needs about 8 kWh. That is why we ask about EV plans at the site visit: it changes the array by 2 to 3 kW and the battery by a module. See battery sizing .

Falls back to off-peak

Cloudy week or a long trip tomorrow, the charger fills from the grid between midnight and 6 am at the cheapest rate on your tariff. You set the departure time, it works backwards.

Is wired on install day

We run the circuit and mount the charger while the switchboard is open, so it costs about $1,700 installed rather than a second call-out. GoodWe HCA 7 kW single-phase or 22 kW three-phase. Sigenergy integrated DC charger 12 to 25 kW on SigenStor systems.

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
