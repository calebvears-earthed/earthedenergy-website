New rules that affect your build · 2026

Four rule changes that shape a residential build

SAPN flexible export
SA Power Networks · updated 2026

Since 2023, all new SA solar installs must connect on the flexible export tariff. Your inverter throttles between 1.5 kW and 10 kW per phase depending on network capacity in real time. On most Hills postcodes you will export near the 10 kW cap most days, but the flexibility exists so the grid stays stable during high-generation moments.
See SAPN flexible export.

SWER supply cap lifted
Rural and Hills · July 2025

Single-wire earth return properties, common in the outer Hills, had a historic 5 kVA solar limit. Since July 2025, SWER customers can install up to 10 kVA of solar and 10 kVA of battery. If you are on a SWER supply, a properly sized system is finally possible without a private line upgrade.
See SWER lines.

CHB battery rebate tier maths
Federal · current 2026

The Cheaper Home Batteries rebate applies at three tiers: 6.80 STCs per kWh for the first 14 kWh, 4.08 for the next 14 kWh, and 1.02 above that. At current spot pricing, a 25 kWh battery attracts around $5,500 in rebate; a 50 kWh battery around $7,200. Applied directly to your invoice up front, no paperwork on your side.
See federal rebates explained.

NETCC compliance
New Energy Tech Consumer Code

Every Earthed quote and install runs under the NETCC framework: 10-business-day cooling-off, plain-English contract terms, defined dispute resolution pathway. If a competitor's proposal does not reference NETCC, ask why.
