Why Earthed?

Renewable home energy specialists, South Australia wide.

Earthed was built by Caleb Vears, Adelaide Hills-born, with twelve years across battery storage, wind, solar and utility construction. We design and install solar, battery storage, EV charging, air conditioning and hot water for homes and businesses across SA. One trade for five systems, sized to what you actually use, and built by the same team that quoted it.

Hand-picked gear, honest prices

A select range of Tier-1 panels and batteries we would put on our own homes, priced without the retailer margin. Real warranties, backed by us.

Utility-scale DNA

The crew that joints HV cable on wind farms and commissions 111 MW batteries installs your system. Licensed, CEC-accredited, SAPN-accredited.

Power when the grid drops out

Battery storage is our specialty. Whole-home backup with sub-second switchover, and evening solar coverage so the bill stays near zero.

Local, and still here in year ten

Adelaide Hills based, all of SA covered. 10-year workmanship warranty, lifetime monitoring, and aftercare with a real phone and a real person. No sales silliness.

Every Earthed job runs the same way: we listen first, do the maths second, and walk every site in person before a single number lands on paper. No call-centre quoting, no roof-size guesswork. Designed around what your home actually draws, your budget and where your energy use is heading over the next ten years — then built, commissioned and handed over by the same team that designed it.
