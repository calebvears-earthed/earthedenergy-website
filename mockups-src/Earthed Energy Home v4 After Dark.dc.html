Rebates Explained

Between the federal STC scheme and the Cheaper Home Batteries program, 10–20% of most systems is covered before you pay a cent. Both are calculated on your written quote and deducted from the price — no forms, no reimbursement chasing.

Solar · STCs

Small-scale Technology Certificates on every CEC-approved panel and inverter. Applied as a point-of-sale discount on the quote.

FEDERAL · APPLIED UPFRONT
Battery · Cheaper Home Batteries

Federal rebate on home battery storage, new or retrofit. Roughly 30% off the battery portion, deducted before you pay.

FEDERAL · NEW OR RETROFIT
Hot water · Heat pump STCs

Heat pump hot water attracts its own STCs. Net installed cost lands around $4,000–6,000 after the rebate.

FEDERAL · APPLIED UPFRONT
