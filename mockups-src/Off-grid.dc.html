Backup generator · sized and supplied
Included in every tier

The genset is part of the design, not a bolt-on later.

Every Earthed off-grid build includes a diesel backup genset in the delivered price, sized against the system. For small-scale builds we supply the Pramac 5.9 kVA silenced diesel with a 2-wire auto-start controller, the market leader in the silenced class and quiet enough for a neighbourly property. For family homestead builds we supply the MaxWatt 10 kVA with built-in auto-start, three-phase output, matched to the inverter platform.

The genset auto-starts when the battery drops below a configurable threshold, runs long enough to bring the battery back to a safe state of charge, then shuts down. In practice on a well-sized system in South Australia, the genset runs a total of 20 to 40 hours per year, mostly during the June-July cloud stretch and after multi-day summer storms.

New rules that affect your build · 2026

Five rules that shape an off-grid build

Off-grid battery rebate exclusion
CHB · grid-connect only

The federal Cheaper Home Batteries rebate applies to grid-connected battery installs only. Off-grid battery systems do not qualify. The solar-side STC rebate still applies fully. Worth knowing before you compare an off-grid quote to a grid-connect one · the maths are different.
See off-grid vs grid-connect.

Bushfire-prone area design
BAL zones · SA · regularly updated

Much of the Adelaide Hills and regional SA sits in designated bushfire-prone areas. This affects component selection (labelling, isolation, fire-rated cable routing), battery cabinet location (setback from combustibles), and emergency access. Every off-grid design accounts for the site's BAL zone from the outset.
See bushfire prone areas.

CEC off-grid design accreditation
Separate from grid-connect design

CEC off-grid solar PV design is a separate national accreditation from grid-connect design. Most solar installers do not hold it. Earthed has an in-house off-grid electrician with 30-plus years of experience and all the relevant accreditations to manage your project end to end.

Sigenergy off-grid firmware
Paid upgrade · Sigenergy premium tier only

The Sigenergy off-grid firmware upgrade is a paid activation on top of the standard SigenStor platform. It unlocks the inverter’s grid-forming capability plus the Sigen Gateway’s islanding and generator-input features. Confirm current upgrade pricing at quote stage. Not required for the GoodWe ES or Selectronic tiers; they are off-grid natively.

Selectronic Australian-made premium
Selectronic · Melbourne-made · world-class

The Selectronic SP PRO 2i is Australian-designed and manufactured in Melbourne. It is considered the world’s best off-grid inverter by expert industry reviewers. Twenty-year hardware warranty, multi-phase capable, fast integration with managed lithium batteries. Priced 20 to 30 percent higher than the equivalent Sigenergy or GoodWe. Worth it for permanent-occupancy remote properties where reliability and long-term serviceability are the priorities.
