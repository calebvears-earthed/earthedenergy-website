Our approach

Five calm steps. No hard sell.

You would not let a stranger build your roof. We do not think you should let one build your energy system either.

01 · Listen
Free energy audit

We sit down with your bill, your usage and your plans. No pressure, just an honest read on what your home actually needs.

02 · Design
Home assessment

We design the system around your roof, your budget and your finance options, and explain every step before anything is ordered.

03 · Approve
Grid and rebates

SAPN application, STC paperwork and rebate claims are lodged by us. You never chase a single form.

04 · Install
Installation day

Caleb or Troy meets the crew on site and walks the job with you. Any brief power outage is flagged well in advance.

05 · Live
Commissioning and care

System tested and app paired, then full technical support stays on hand. We are one phone call away, for good.
