The middle path

Hybrid with backup

A grid-connected hybrid with a Gateway or built-in transfer switch gives you the grid as a cheap backup generator and a battery that carries the house when the grid drops. You keep the feed-in income and the rebates, you lose the anxiety. It costs a third of a true off-grid system.

True off-grid is for where the grid is not there, or costs more to bring in than the system does. See outage backup .

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
