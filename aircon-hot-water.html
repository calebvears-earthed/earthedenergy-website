Aircon Hot Water

Heating, cooling and hot water are about 60 percent of a Hills bill. Solar only helps if they run when the sun does.

Hot water first

Reclaim Energy CO₂ heat pump

A quarter of the energy

A resistive tank uses 8 to 10 kWh a day for a family. A Reclaim CO₂ heat pump does the same job on about 2 kWh, because it moves heat rather than making it.

Timed to midday

We set it to run 11 am to 3 pm on solar. Hot water for the night costs nothing. Controlled load overnight becomes unnecessary.

Kills the gas bill

If it replaces gas, the daily gas supply charge goes with it, around $250 a year before you heat a single litre. 6-year warranty. 250, 315 and 400 litre tanks.

Then heating and cooling

Daikin Alira X split and ducted

Sized to the room, not the floor plan

We size against insulation, glazing and orientation, not square metres. Hills homes with big north glass and no ceiling insulation need very different units from a new build in Mount Barker.

Runs on daytime solar

Pre-cool or pre-heat the house from 1 pm to 5 pm on your own production and the battery carries the evening. A 2.5 kW Daikin split draws about 0.6 kW steady state. Heating for cents.

One brand, one controller

Daikin Alira X splits, multi-head and ducted reverse-cycle, five-star, five-year full warranty. Whole-house zoning if you want it. Installed by the same crew as the solar.

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
