What goes on your roof and wall

Panels

Trina Vertex S+ 475 W default, Astronergy 470 W budget. 25-year product, 30-year output.

Inverters

GoodWe ESA single-phase, GoodWe ET Plus three-phase, Sigenergy SigenStor SP and TP. Ten-year warranties.

Batteries

GoodWe Lynx and Sigenergy SigenStor modules. LFP, modular, 10 years to 70 percent.

Racking and cable

Clenergy racking to AS/NZS 1170.2. Nexans and Prysmian cable. Stäubli MC4 connectors.

EV charging

GoodWe HCA AC charger or Sigenergy integrated DC. Solar-priority. Wired on install day.

Aircon and hot water

Daikin Alira X reverse-cycle. Reclaim Energy CO₂ heat pump hot water timed to midday solar.

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
