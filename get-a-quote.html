The $13,999* and $19,999* figures (and the $2,999* EV charger) are indicative package prices for a straightforward single-storey home in greater Adelaide, after the rebates we are able to apply to the invoice. They are not a quotation. Roof type, switchboard condition, metering, access and the final design can change the price. Nothing is ordered until you have a written quote.

The federal Cheaper Home Batteries discount is approximately 30% via STCs. It varies with usable capacity and install date, and steps down from 1 January 2027. Your quote shows the figure that applies. We do not lock a dollar amount on this page.
