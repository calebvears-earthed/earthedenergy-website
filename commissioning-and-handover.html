What we do before we leave

Electrical testing

Insulation resistance on every string, polarity, earth continuity, RCD trip times, and the anti-islanding test that proves the inverter shuts down when the grid does. Results go on the Certificate of Compliance you receive.

Backup test

If you have backup, we pull the main switch and show you the house stay on. Then we show you what does not run on battery, so there are no surprises in the first storm.

App setup

SEMS or mySigen installed on your phone, account created, tariff entered, backup reserve set. We show you the three numbers to check weekly. See monitoring apps .

Paperwork

Certificate of Compliance, SAPN connection documents, every manufacturer warranty registered in your name, the Clenergy engineering certificate, and our 10-year workmanship warranty. Printed and emailed.

One number

Caleb's mobile. 0433 839 284. Not a support portal. If something looks wrong on the app in year four, that is who you call.

After we leave

Week one to month six

Installer access on the monitoring portal means we see your production alongside every other system we have installed. If yours is under-performing on a clear day, we usually know before you do. Most first-month calls are us ringing you.

At six months we come back for a complimentary panel clean and a system health check. That is also when we look at whether your usage has changed and whether a battery module or a hot water change would help. See warranty and aftercare .

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
