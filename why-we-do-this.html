Why We Do This

It is Caleb explaining why the business exists, in his own words.

Written September 2026

We have more rooftop solar per head than almost anywhere on earth, and a lot of it was sold by people who never saw the roof. I have pulled panels off Hills homes that were racked into the sheet instead of the purlin, wired with connectors that were never crimped, and sized for a rebate rather than a family. The owners had no idea. The installer had stopped answering the phone.

I spent twelve years on the utility side where none of that would survive a single inspection. When we started Earthed Energy the idea was not complicated: do residential the way we do high voltage. Walk the site. Design it properly. Install it ourselves with the good cable. Stay on the phone afterwards.

What we are trying to change

We publish the maths behind our calculator. We publish which battery we would pick for which house and why. We tell people when a battery will not pay back on their bill. None of that is good marketing. It is just what you would want from the person wiring your house, and it has turned out to be good business anyway, because Hills people talk to each other.

If reading this site gets you a better system from someone else, that is fine. If it gets you a better system from us, that is better. Either way, ring me if you have a question. 0433 839 284.

Solar by day. Battery by night. The bill becomes a memory. That is the whole pitch, and it should be true.

Caleb Vears · Director
