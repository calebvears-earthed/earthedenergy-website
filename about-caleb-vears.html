Where it started

Goyder South · Nyrstar · Barossa BESS

I started jointing 33 kV cable on Goyder South Wind Farm near Burra, 78 turbines, for Neoen and their contractors. That is where you learn that a connection either holds for thirty years or fails in the first storm, and there is nothing in between. From there it was the HV switch room rebuild at Nyrstar Port Pirie after a cable fire, a 5 MW solar farm at Port Wakefield, and operating the 111 MW battery in the Barossa.

Twelve years of that gives you a particular view of a residential switchboard. It is small, but the physics is identical and the consequences of getting it wrong land on a family, not a utility.

Earthed High Voltage is the utility business. Earthed Energy exists because people I knew in the Hills kept getting quotes from call centres for systems designed off a satellite photo, installed by a subcontractor they never met, with a warranty that pointed at a phone tree. I knew we could do it properly, ourselves, with the same cable and the same care we use at 33 kV.

So we do. I walk the roof or Troy does. We install it ourselves. My mobile is on every page of this site because that is the aftercare model: a person, not a portal.

We build the equation that lets the sun run your home. Then we stay on the phone.

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
