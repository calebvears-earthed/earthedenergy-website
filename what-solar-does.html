What Solar Does

No physics lecture. Just what changes at your place from the first sunny morning.

Six things

What solar actually does for a South Australian home.

1

Save on your bill.

A properly sized system drops a $600/quarter bill to around $150 in the first year. That's $1,800 back in your pocket every year.

2

Less grid, more independence.

You buy less power at the peak evening rate. When the grid drops, your battery keeps the lights on if it's wired for backup.

3

Adds property value.

A commercial-grade solar + battery install on the roof adds $15,000 to $25,000 to a home's sale price. The rebate stays with the property.

4

The rebate goes on your invoice.

Federal STC and CHB both come off the sticker price up front. You never see a form.

5

Cleaner power in your walls.

More of what you use comes from your own roof. Less from a coal-heavy grid two states away.

6

Panels last 25+ years. Batteries 10+.

Inverter needs a swap around year 12-15. That's the maintenance schedule. Everything else looks after itself.
