A standard 10 kW plus battery install

Arrive and set up

Our team likes to start early. We meet you on site to go over the day and the safety requirements. This is where we find out whether the dog bites. By this point the crew is registering the equipment on site and preparing for installation.

Solar panel installation

Racking goes on first. Every foot screwed to a purlin or batten, sealed, and the rail levelled. Panels follow, each one clipped, each connector crimped and pull-tested. DC cable runs in conduit to the isolator.

Wall and switchboard

Inverter and battery mounted, usually near the meter box. Switchboard work: new breakers, the backup changeover or Gateway, energy meter, and labelling. Power is off to the house for one to two hours here. We tell you when.

Commissioning

System energised, inverter configured for your SAPN export arrangement, battery balanced, app set up on your phone. Day two, if needed, is for larger arrays, three-phase boards or bushfire-zone conduit runs.

Handover

We walk you through the system, the app and the backup circuits, hand over the compliance pack, and book your six-month panel clean and health check before we leave.

Access · safety · noise

Access. We need to get to the roof, the meter box and the battery location without moving your furniture. If there is a dog, a locked gate or a neighbour's driveway involved, tell us the day before. We also need about 8 metres of kerb or driveway for the van and trailer.

Safety. Edge protection and harnesses go up before anyone steps on the roof. Please keep children and pets clear of the work area and the ladder, and expect the power to be off to the house for one to two hours while we work on the switchboard.

Noise. Panelling is the loud part of the day: drilling into purlins, racking going on, feet on the roof. We coordinate this with you beforehand so you can plan around it, whether that is a work-from-home call or a baby's nap.

You do not need to be home the whole day, but we want you there for the last hour so we can hand over properly. See commissioning and handover .
