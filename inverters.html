Equipment · Inverters

Inverters

Three inverter families. Which one you get depends on your phases, your battery plan and your backup needs.

On this page

  1. Quick selector

Single-phase · 5 to 10 kW · all-in-one

The ESA is a single-phase hybrid with the Lynx battery modules stacked underneath it and the backup transfer switch built in. One wall footprint, one install, one app. Available at 5, 8 and 10 kW with up to 13 kW of panels on the 10. Backup output matches the inverter rating with about a 30 percent derate on motor loads.

It is our default for single-phase homes on bills up to about $800 a quarter. Ten-year inverter warranty, ten-year battery warranty.

GoodWe ET Plus

Three-phase · 5 to 15 kW · hybrid

Where the house is three-phase, the ET Plus takes the ESA's job. It is a wall-mounted three-phase hybrid from 5 to 15 kW with two or three MPPT inputs, up to 200 percent panel oversizing, and it pairs with the same Lynx battery modules. Backup is via an external changeover and covers the phases we wire it to.

Ten-year warranty. Common on Hills acreage with ducted aircon and a shed on its own phase.

SP 3 to 12 kW · TP 5 to 25 kW

SigenStor stacks the inverter, battery modules, energy management and an optional DC EV charger into one column. Single-phase SP models run 3 to 12 kW, three-phase TP models 5 to 25 kW. Battery modules are 5 or 8 kWh and stack to 48 kWh. With the Gateway it delivers zero-millisecond whole-home backup with no derate.

It costs more than GoodWe and it does more. We spec it for three-phase homes, EV owners, and anyone who wants the house to feel completely normal in a blackout. Our rule: no Sigenergy without a Gateway .
