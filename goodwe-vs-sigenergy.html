GoodWe vs Sigenergy

We install both. Here is how we compare the two.

On this page

  1. The differences that decide it

Why two platforms

We commonly see retailers and installers chasing whichever equipment carries the best margin or wholesaler deal that month, and the brand on the quote changes with it. We come from a technical background, and putting a 10-year workmanship warranty behind a system is a risk we want to take with confidence. That means two industry-respected brands we know inside out, so if an issue ever arises there is no conflict between manufacturer and installer over who owns it. GoodWe covers most homes at a sharper price point. Sigenergy sits at a higher price point and earns it with zero-millisecond backup and native EV charging. Both do their own respective jobs brilliantly in day-to-day operation, ease of installation and monitoring.

Both are Tier 1 manufacturers with Australian warranty offices, both are LFP chemistry, both carry ten-year warranties on inverter and battery, and both talk to SAPN Flexible Exports. We have installed and serviced both. We would put either on our own house.
