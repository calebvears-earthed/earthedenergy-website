About Troy Hooker

CEC-Accredited Designer via Troy's Electrical. SAA A3996068. More than ten years of solar, nothing else.

Design · sign-off · compliance

The STC rebate and the Cheaper Home Batteries rebate both require a CEC-accredited designer to sign the system design. Troy is ours. Every quote we issue has been through his shade model, his string calculation and his inverter sizing before it has a price on it.

He has been designing residential and small commercial solar in South Australia for more than a decade, through every change in SAPN export rules, panel wattage and battery chemistry. He is the reason we spec two MPPTs on a treed block and the reason we do not oversize a battery against a small array.

How we work together

Caleb and Troy

Site visits are Caleb or Troy. Designs are Troy. Installs are Caleb's crew with Troy checking the commissioning data. It is a two-person quality loop on every job, which is more than most installers of any size can say.

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
