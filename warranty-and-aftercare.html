The four warranties

What covers what

WarrantyTermCoversWho honours it
Earthed workmanship10 yearsRacking, roof penetrations, cabling, switchboard work, water ingress from our work, labour on any warranty replacementUs, directly
Panels (Trina)25 yr product, 30 yr outputPanel defects, output below 87% at year 30Trina Australia, we handle the claim
Inverter (GoodWe or Sigenergy)10 yearsInverter failure, firmware, transfer switchManufacturer, we handle the claim and the swap
Battery (Lynx or SigenStor)10 years to 70% capacityModule failure, capacity below 70%Manufacturer, we handle the claim and the swap

We moved from 7 to 10 years on workmanship in 2026. Every quote states all four terms in years.

The honest scenario

Inverters are the component most likely to need attention inside the warranty. Typical failure is a fan, a relay or a firmware fault at year 6 to 10. You see it on the app, or we do. You ring Caleb. We come out, diagnose, and if it is a warranty fault we lodge the claim and swap the unit. You pay nothing. The labour is covered by our workmanship warranty even when the fault is the manufacturer's.

Out of warranty, a service call is charged at our standard rate, quoted before we come. We do not do "site fee to look at it".

Six months and beyond

At six months we come back, clean the panels, check every connector and roof penetration, review your app data against your bill, and tell you if anything should change. After that we are your energy point of contact: EV charger, heat pump, more battery, a shed, whatever comes next.

Lifetime monitoring access. No subscription. No portal. One mobile number.

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
