Why Earthed?

Earthed brings extensive experience and deep knowledge to the renewable energy field, and we take pride in being a high-quality, full service for all renewable electrical needs. A boutique team with honest advice and strict start-to-finish processes that cut out the common sales silliness in the industry.

Our Process

Six steps, the same every time. We listen first, do the maths second, and walk every site in person before a price is put to paper. No call-centre quoting, no roof-size guesswork — a system sized to what you use, not what fits your roof.

01
Listen

Where you’re located, and your main interests and concerns.

02
Study + Walk

We do the maths from your bill and daily yield, talk through your energy plans, and size a system to suit. Site inspection on every job.

03
Solve

Sized to what you use, your financial situation and long-term energy plans.

04
Propose

Price, clearly outlined terms and conditions, warranty information and signing. Materials ordered the same day the deposit lands.

05
Coordinate the build

Install date booked, wiring plans finalised. SAPN, STC, CEC-accredited team under HSEQ procedures. 3–5 week turnaround.

06
Aftercare

6-month revisit for complimentary panel cleaning and a system health assessment. We become your energy point of contact.

Rebates Explained

Between the federal STC scheme and the Cheaper Home Batteries program, 10–20% of most systems is covered before you pay a cent. Both are calculated on your written quote and deducted from the price — no forms, no reimbursement chasing.

Solar · STCs

Small-scale Technology Certificates on every CEC-approved panel and inverter. Applied as a point-of-sale discount on the quote.

FEDERAL · APPLIED UPFRONT
Battery · Cheaper Home Batteries

Federal rebate on home battery storage, new or retrofit. Roughly 30% off the battery portion, deducted before you pay.

FEDERAL · NEW OR RETROFIT
Hot water · Heat pump STCs

Heat pump hot water attracts its own STCs. Net installed cost lands around $4,000–6,000 after the rebate.
