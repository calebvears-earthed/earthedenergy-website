Racking and Cable

Racking, cable, isolators and connectors. Boring, in a good way.

Racking
Clenergy · AS/NZS 1170.2 · wind region A

Clenergy is an Australian company and their racking is certified to AS/NZS 1170.2 for our wind regions. On Colorbond we use their tin-roof interface with an EPDM seal under every foot, screwed into the purlin, not the sheet. On tile we use a tile bracket with a flashed hook. Every job gets a site-specific engineering certificate from Clenergy's calculator for your roof pitch, height and exposure.

Hills ridgelines are wind region A but often terrain category 2 with full exposure. That means more feet, closer spacing, and mid-clamps rated for it. We do not stretch the spacing to save an hour.

Cable
Nexans · Prysmian · double-insulated PV1-F

Our background is high voltage: cable work under SA Power Networks regulations and on private-sector wind and solar farms, where the standards are stricter than any rooftop. That is the discipline we bring to your roof, along with strong relationships and supply from Nexans and Prysmian, the brands we are certified on. DC cable is double-insulated 6 mm² PV1-F rated to 1500 V and 120 °C, UV-stable, in conduit wherever it is not directly under a panel.

Connectors are genuine Stäubli MC4, crimped with the Stäubli tool, never the generic look-alikes that account for most rooftop DC fires in Australia. Every connector gets a pull test before the panel goes on.

All of it comes through wholesalers we have dealt with for years: Tradezone, OSW, MM Electrical and Energy Spurt. Genuine stock, traceable batches, and someone who picks up the phone when a warranty claim needs a hand.

Isolation and labelling
AS/NZS 5033

DC isolators sit at the inverter and, where the standard requires, at the array. We use IP66 enclosures with a shroud, mounted where a firefighter on the ground can see and reach them. Labels are engraved traffolyte, not printed stickers, because Adelaide UV kills a sticker in two summers.
