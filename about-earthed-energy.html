About Earthed Energy

Trading as Earthed High Voltage Pty Ltd. Adelaide Hills based. All of South Australia covered.

The company

Earthed High Voltage Pty Ltd does utility work: HV cable jointing, switching, commissioning and battery operations for SA Power Networks, wind farm contractors and industrial sites. Earthed Energy is the same company, the same licence and the same people, doing residential and small commercial solar, battery, EV charging, air conditioning and hot water.

We are based in the Adelaide Hills and we work across all of South Australia, from Mount Barker to Fowlers Bay. Post goes to PO Box 4111, Norwood South SA 5067.
