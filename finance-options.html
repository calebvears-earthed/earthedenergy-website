Finance Options

Cash, interest-free, or a green loan. We explain the dealer fee because most people do not.

On this page

  1. A $28,000 system three ways

Simplest path, shortest payback. A $30,000 system becomes $27,900. Deposit on signing, balance on commissioning. Most of our Hills customers pay this way.

Elevant is a solar-specific lender. Under $10,000, the loan is genuinely 0 percent to you, with terms from 12 to 60 months and weekly, fortnightly or monthly repayments. Online application, approval in hours.

Here is the catch nobody prints: interest-free finance is funded by a dealer fee that the lender charges us, typically 8 to 15 percent of the amount financed. So a $10,000 interest-free system costs us more to sell than a $10,000 cash system, and the 7 percent cash discount does not apply. We tell you that up front so you can compare the real numbers.

For systems above $10,000, 28Watt places a personal green loan with a bank at 4 to 7 percent over 3 to 7 years. No dealer fee, so the cash discount survives. Repayments on a $28,000 system over 7 years land around $95 a week, which is roughly what a $1,000-a-quarter bill was costing.

Approval is subject to normal credit checks. We are not the lender and we do not see your financials.
