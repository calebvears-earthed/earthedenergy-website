Solar Panels

Panels are the least likely part of your system to fail. We still choose carefully.

Default
Trina Vertex S+ · NEG9R.28 · 475 W

Trina is a Tier 1 manufacturer that has been shipping panels since 1997 and is on every bankability list that matters. The Vertex S+ is their residential panel: n-type TOPCon cells, glass on both faces so there is no plastic backsheet to yellow, 475 W in a 1762 × 1134 mm frame, about 22 percent efficient. Full black, so it disappears on a dark roof.

Warranty is 25 years on the product and 30 years on output, guaranteed to at least 87 percent of rated power in year 30. Degradation is under 0.4 percent a year. It is the panel on most of our Hills installs.

Budget
Astronergy ASTRO N7 · 470 W

Astronergy (Chint) makes a very similar n-type TOPCon panel at 470 W with about 21.5 percent efficiency. It is Tier 1, CEC-listed, and the output warranty is the same 30 years. The product warranty is 15 years rather than 25 and the frame is silver rather than black on some models. It saves roughly $30 to $40 a panel installed.

On a 28-panel roof that is about $1,000. If the budget is tight and the roof is not visible from the street, it is a sensible saving. If you want the black roof and the 25-year product cover, Trina.
