Four rule changes that shape a residential build

SAPN flexible export

SA Power Networks · updated 2026

Since 2023, all new SA solar installs must connect on the flexible export tariff. Your inverter throttles between 1.5 kW and 10 kW per phase depending on network capacity in real time. On most Hills postcodes you will export near the 10 kW cap most days, but the flexibility exists so the grid stays stable during high-generation moments. See SAPN flexible export .

SWER supply cap lifted

Rural and Hills · July 2025

Single-wire earth return properties, common in the outer Hills, had a historic 5 kVA solar limit. Since July 2025, SWER customers can install up to 10 kVA of solar and 10 kVA of battery. If you are on a SWER supply, a properly sized system is finally possible without a private line upgrade. See SWER lines .

CHB battery rebate tier maths

Federal · current 2026

Cheaper Home Batteries

The federal Cheaper Home Batteries discount is paid as small-scale technology certificates (STCs). It comes to approximately 30% of the eligible battery cost. The exact discount varies with usable capacity and the installation date, and the rate steps down from 1 January 2027. We calculate it on your written quote. We do not publish a fixed dollar amount.

New Energy Tech Consumer Code

Every Earthed quote and install runs under the NETCC framework: 10-business-day cooling-off, plain-English contract terms, defined dispute resolution pathway. If a competitor's proposal does not reference NETCC, ask why.
