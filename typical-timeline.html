Realistic ranges, not best case

Day 0 · sign and deposit

Contract signed, 10-business-day cooling-off starts. Deposit lands, materials ordered the same afternoon.

Day 1 to 3 · SAPN application

We lodge the connection application with SA Power Networks: inverter model, export arrangement, phases. Straightforward single-phase Flexible Exports approvals come back in 5 to 10 business days.

Day 5 to 20 · materials

Trina panels and GoodWe are usually ex-stock Adelaide, one to two weeks. Sigenergy and specific battery module counts can run three to four weeks. We tell you the lead time on the quote.

Day 10 to 30 · SAPN approval

SWER, three-phase over 15 kW, or a rural transformer check can add two to four weeks. We chase it weekly and tell you what we hear.

Day 20 to 45 · install

One to two days on site once materials and approval line up. We book you two weeks out and confirm the week before.

Install day + 0 to 10 · meter and retailer

If a new meter is needed, your retailer schedules it, typically within 10 business days. Solar runs from install day; the feed-in credit starts when the meter does.

Install day + 6 months · aftercare visit

Panel clean and health check. See warranty and aftercare .

Figures in these guides are indicative. The price, and any rebate, that applies to your house is the one on the written quote.
