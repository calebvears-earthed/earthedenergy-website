New rules that affect your build · 2026

Five rules that shape a commercial build

100 kW STC threshold
Small-scale vs large-scale · federal

Systems up to 100 kW claim STCs, paid up front and applied to the invoice. Systems above 100 kW claim LGCs, an annual generation-based credit paid over the life of the system. The optimal size for most SA commercial rooftops with a three-phase supply is 99 kW, right at the STC ceiling. We will model both if your load supports something bigger.

Small business instant asset write-off
ATO · eligible assets under threshold

For eligible small businesses, the solar system can be written off in the year of install rather than depreciated over its life. This dramatically shortens the after-tax payback period. Threshold and eligibility change with each federal budget · confirm current rules with your accountant before signing.

SAPN commercial connection
SA Power Networks · commercial

Commercial connections above 30 kW require an engineered application and witness inspection. Timeline: 4 to 8 weeks from application to approval. We handle every step, including the network capacity study if required for your suburb.
See SAPN flexible export.

NETCC and AS/NZS 3000 commercial
Wiring standards and consumer code

Commercial installs run under the same NETCC framework as residential, plus AS/NZS 3000 wiring compliance and AS/NZS 5033 solar-specific standards. Every install is signed off by a licensed contractor. Our licence: PGE 336477.

Ownership and tenancy structures
Landlord and tenant solar on commercial premises

Solar on a leased commercial roof needs the landlord's permission, because the rebate is tied to the property, not the tenant. We have handled tenant-initiated, landlord-initiated and shared-cost structures, including 50/50 arrangements for shared-benefit commercial solar.
