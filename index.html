Rebates

What comes off the price.

You don't lodge the paperwork. We apply every eligible discount to the invoice. The package figures above already assume that. The final number is on your written quote.

Auto-applied · Federal solar

STC rebate

Small-scale Technology Certificates. We register the install with the Clean Energy Regulator and discount the certificate value off the invoice. Currently about $400/kW for SA Zone 3 on a five-year deeming period. Indicative only.

Auto-applied · Federal battery

Cheaper Home Batteries

The federal battery discount is paid as small-scale technology certificates (STCs). It is approximately 30% of the eligible battery cost. It varies with usable capacity and the installation date, and the rate steps down from 1 January 2027. We do not promise a fixed dollar amount.

Optional · SA Power Networks

Virtual power plant

Opt in to a compatible VPP and earn credits on top of bill savings. Ask which inverter and battery combinations qualify. This is separate from the federal discount.

Rebate values move with certificate prices and the rules on the day of installation. The figure that applies to you is the one on the written quote.
