Battery Storage

Two module families. Both stack. Both carry ten-year warranties. Here are the numbers.

On this page

  1. Side by side

LFP · modular · 10-year warranty

Lynx modules are lithium iron phosphate, the chemistry that does not do thermal runaway the way older lithium cobalt cells did. Modules run around 5 to 6.5 kWh usable each and stack to roughly 26 kWh on a single inverter, more with a second cluster. IP66, wall or floor mount, rated for outdoor Hills weather.

Warranty is 10 years or a stated throughput, whichever comes first, to 70 percent retained capacity. On a normal one-cycle-a-day household the ten years runs out first.

Sigenergy SigenStor BAT

LFP · 5 or 8 kWh · to 48 kWh

SigenStor battery modules are also LFP, in 5 kWh and 8 kWh sizes, with active cell balancing and a fire-suppression aerosol built into each module. They stack directly under the inverter head, so a 3-module 24 kWh system is one clean column about 1.6 metres tall.

Ten-year warranty to 70 percent retained capacity. Adding capacity later is a two-hour job: unbolt the top, slide in a module, re-commission from the app.
